# 🛤️ Linked List Mastery Roadmap

### **Phase 1 – Foundation**

* ✅ What is a Linked List?

  * Node structure (`data`, `next` pointer).
  * Difference vs Arrays (contiguous memory vs dynamic).
  * When to use LL (frequent insert/delete).

* ✅ **Singly Linked List Basics**

  * Create a Node class.
  * Initialize `head`.
  * Traverse & print list.
  * Count nodes.

---

### **Phase 2 – Core Operations**

Practice implementing each one (not just memorizing):

* Insert at **beginning**
* Insert at **end**
* Insert at **position / after node**
* Insert before node (slightly trickier)
* Delete **first node**
* Delete **last node**
* Delete node by **value**
* Search element in list

👉 These are your bread & butter for 90% of problems.

---

### **Phase 3 – Intermediate Workouts**

Now handle common interview-style operations:

* Reverse a linked list (iterative & recursive).
* Find middle node (slow & fast pointer).
* Detect cycle (Floyd’s cycle detection).
* Remove duplicates (sorted & unsorted).
* Merge two sorted linked lists.
* Intersection of two linked lists.

---

### **Phase 4 – Advanced Problems**

Push yourself here:

* Detect & remove cycle.
* Rotate linked list (by k).
* Partition list around a value (like quicksort).
* Check if list is palindrome.
* Add two numbers represented by linked lists.
* Flatten a multilevel linked list.

---

### **Phase 5 – Doubly & Circular Linked List**

* **Doubly Linked List (DLL)**:

  * Node has `prev` and `next`.
  * Insertions/deletions in DLL.
* **Circular Linked List**:

  * Last node points back to head.
  * Traversals + insert/delete.

👉 You don’t need to go super deep here unless interviewer asks. Most focus is on **singly LL**.

---

### **Phase 6 – Practice & LeetCode Grind**

Target problems:

* Easy: Reverse Linked List, Merge Two Sorted Lists, Remove Duplicates.
* Medium: Linked List Cycle, Intersection, Add Two Numbers.
* Hard: Reverse Nodes in k-Group, Copy List with Random Pointer.

---

### **Phase 7 – System Thinking**

* Applications of Linked List:

  * Implementing Stacks & Queues.
  * Adjacency list in Graphs.
  * Memory management (free lists).
* Complexity analysis:

  * Why insert/delete O(1), search O(n).

---

⚡ **Tip for mastery:**
Every time you solve a problem, **do both iterative & recursive versions**. That doubles your understanding.
